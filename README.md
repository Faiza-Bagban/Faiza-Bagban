### 🗣️ Neural Speech Disfluency Detection & Remediation
> *Clinical-grade stutter detection via self-supervised transformer fine-tuning.*

<table>
<tr>
<td width="60%">

**Problem:** Real-world disfluency detection demands robustness across noisy acoustic conditions and precise millisecond-level classification — far beyond what standard ASR pipelines offer.

**Innovation:** Fine-tuned Wav2Vec 2.0 on SEP-28k (16kHz) with Whisper-based silver-standard labeling + 12th-order LPC acoustic evaluation (LLR, ISD) for ground-truth generation.

**Results:**
- 🎯 Precision threshold = **0.93** with millisecond excision
- 📉 WER < **0.15** (Whisper-aligned)
- 🔊 Validated across **5–25 dB SNR** noise conditions
- 📊 MOS evaluation across **n=40** participants
